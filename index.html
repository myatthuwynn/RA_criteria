🔍 ACR/EULAR 2010 RA Classification Criteria

Score ≥6 = DEFINITE RHEUMATOID ARTHRITIS

👤Patient Information

🦴1. Joint Involvement

Small joints: PIP, MCP, wrist (bilaterally). Count any involved joint (tender or swollen).

🧬2. Serology (RF and Anti-CCP)

Rule: Use the HIGHEST value from RF and Anti-CCP. If only one is positive, use that value.

⚗️3. Acute Phase Reactants

If EITHER ESR OR CRP is abnormal = 1 point. Normal values: ESR <20 mm/hr, CRP <10 mg/L

⏱️4. Symptom Duration

Score can be applied at first visit. Symptoms must be ≥6 weeks for disease-modifying therapy.
